Day-by-Day Experience

1
Day 1 • Zurich to Lucerne

Welcome to Switzerland & Lake Lucerne Orientation

expand_more

Arrive at Zurich Kloten Airport where our dedicated Varni Holidays Swiss tour representative greets you. Transfer via comfortable Swiss Rail first-class carriage to Lucerne (approx. 50 mins).

Includes: Private Meet & Greet Overnight: Hotel Radisson Blu Lucerne Dinner: Indian Welcome Buffet
2
Day 2 • Alpine Magic

Mount Titlis Rotair Cable Car & Lucerne Old Town

expand_more

Ascend to 10,000 feet on the world's first revolving cable car, Rotair. Cross the breathtaking Titlis Cliff Walk suspension bridge, step into the Glacier Cave, and enjoy snow activities. Afternoon walk across the historic Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke) and Lion Monument.

Highlights: Titlis Cliff Walk & Ice Flyer Meals: Breakfast & Indian Lunch at Titlis
3
Day 3 • Bernese Oberland

Jungfraujoch - Top of Europe & Interlaken Promenade

expand_more

Board the cutting-edge Eiger Express gondola from Grindelwald terminal, connecting to the historic cogwheel train into Jungfraujoch. Marvel at the Aletsch Glacier, explore the Lindt Chocolate Heaven, and spend late afternoon strolling Interlaken's bustling shops.

Transport: Eiger Express + Cogwheel Overnight: Hotel Lindner Grand Interlaken
4
Day 4 • Scenic Railway

GoldenPass Express, Gstaad & Montreux Riviera

expand_more

Relish the iconic panoramic railway journey as Switzerland shifts from rugged peaks to French-speaking lakeside vineyards. Photo-stop at the legendary Chillon Castle on the shores of Lake Geneva.

Highlight: First-Class Panoramas Overnight: Grand Hotel Suisse Majestic
5
Day 5 • Cross Border to France

High-Speed TGV Lyria to Paris & Evening Seine Illumination

expand_more

Glide into Paris Gare de Lyon in under three hours aboard France’s premier high-speed train. Check in to your central 4-star boutique hotel. In the evening, step aboard our private Bateaux Mouches river cruise witnessing the City of Lights glimmer under twilight.

Includes: TGV First Class Ticket Overnight: Hotel Pullman Paris Tour Eiffel
6
Day 6 • Iconic Paris

Eiffel Tower Summit Access & Guided City Panorama

expand_more

Ascend directly to the summit level of the Eiffel Tower with our VIP escort. Drive along Champs-Élysées, Arc de Triomphe, Place de la Concorde, Opera Garnier, and Notre Dame Cathedral area.

Tour: Deluxe Coach with Hindi/English Guide Special: Summit Ticket Included
7
Day 7 • Choose Your Wonder

Full Day Disneyland Paris (1-Day 2-Parks) OR Palace of Versailles

expand_more

Personalize your day: Families immerse in Disneyland Park and Walt Disney Studios with Hopper pass, or explore the royal Hall of Mirrors and vast fountains of the Sun King at Versailles.

Included: Round-trip shuttle transfers
8
Day 8 • Cultural Leisure

Louvre Museum Masterpieces & Boulevard Haussmann Shopping

expand_more

Dedicated museum pass for the Louvre. Spend the afternoon luxury shopping at Galeries Lafayette and Printemps with tax-free concierge assistance from Varni Holidays.

9
Day 9 • Homeward Bound

Au Revoir Paris & Return Flight to India

expand_more

Enjoy a leisurely Parisian croissant breakfast. Private Mercedes Sprinter transfer to Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) for your return flight to Ahmedabad / Mumbai / Delhi with joyful memories.
