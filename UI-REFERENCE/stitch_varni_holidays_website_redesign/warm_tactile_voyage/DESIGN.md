## Brand & Style

This design system expresses bespoke travel curation, heritage warmth, and effortless leisure. Designed for a luxury holiday agency catering to discerning travelers, families, and cultural explorers, the aesthetic bridges editorial prestige with tactile, organic calm.

The visual direction merges **soft organic claymorphism** with **high-end editorial luxury**:
- **Tactile & Pillow-Soft:** Surfaces are not stark planes; they behave like matte-finished porcelain or pressed linen cards with layered, warm dual-directional ambient light.
- **Editorial Grandeur:** Classic high-contrast editorial serifs evoke vintage luggage tags, literary field guides, and aspirational magazine spreads.
- **Grounded Warmth:** Replaces sterile tech-white with sun-drenched ivory, sea-mist azure, and celebratory saffron-gold undertones.

## Colors

The palette captures oceanic depths meeting golden shoreline warmth against a foundation of natural unbleached cotton and fine ivory card stock.

- **Primary (`#153650` - Deep Navy):** Represents authority, voyage history, and timeless elegance. Used for foundational text, primary navigation frames, strong CTA states, and prominent heading contrast.
- **Secondary (`#246B9B` - Ocean Blue):** Expresses the expanse of coastal getaways and clear skies. Utilized for interactive anchors, active itinerary badges, and contextual highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#E9B95E` - Warm Golden Yellow):** Evokes bespoke hospitality, sunlight, and Indian celebratory warmth. Reserved for star ratings, exclusive badge markers, featured pricing, and radiant micro-interactions.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6F0` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas background, replacing digital white with a restful, glare-free linen tone. Complemented by `surface-cream-subtle` (`#F1EEE5`) for recessed surfaces.

### Usage Guidance
- Never combine pure `#000000` text with `#F8F6F0`; always set high-emphasis copy in Primary `#153650`.
- Warm claymorphic surfaces derive their illusion through the interplay of `#FFFFFF` high-angle specular highlights and tinted `#153650` warm-diffused drops.

## Typography

The typographic system balances the poise of Italian serif craft (**Playfair Display**) with the crisp, humanist readability of modern geometric sans (**Plus Jakarta Sans**).

- **Playfair Display:** Carries the emotional and aspirational weight of the agency. Used for destination headliners, showcase quotes, pricing leads, and promotional hero titles. The letterforms invoke high-end hospitality magazines and classical journey memoirs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Balances readability across itineraries, passport forms, tour day-by-day breakdowns, and interactive selectors. The rounded terminals mirror the soft, pillowy shape language of the UI.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an open, breathing grid structured to allow travel imagery and editorial typography to coexist without visual clutter:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px to 1380px maximum content constraint, centered. Outer margins set at `margin: 3rem`, with `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container with 2rem side margins and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid container with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

Rhythm is deliberate: generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) between curated travel collections creates a stately, calm cadence reminiscent of flipping through an oversized luxury coffee-table catalogue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use harsh drop shadows or flat corporate borders. Depth is realized through **organic claymorphic extrusion** and **pillowed diffusion**:

1. **Clay Raised Base (Cards, Hero Panels):**
   - Upper highlight: `inset 2px 2px 4px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
   - Soft outer drop: `8px 16px 28px rgba(21, 54, 80, 0.06), -6px -6px 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`
   - Surface background: `#F8F6F0` to `#FFFFFF` subtle vertical sheen.

2. **Clay Recessed Base (Search Bars, Inset Inputs, Active Tabs):**
   - Inner shade: `inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(21, 54, 80, 0.08), inset -3px -3px 6px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
   - Surface background: `#F1EEE5`.

3. **Floating Floating Elements (Booking Modals, Sticky Booking Drawer):**
   - High-diffusion oceanic drop: `16px 32px 48px rgba(21, 54, 80, 0.12), -4px -4px 20px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
   - Subtle outer boundary line: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, pebble-like organic radii. Softly sculpted silhouettes reflect natural coastal erosion, smooth sea glass, and comfortable physical ergonomics.

- **Primary Radius:** Generous 1rem (`16px`) minimum base for small controls and inputs.
- **Card & Frame Radii:** Sculpted at 2rem (`32px`) to 2.5rem (`40px`), giving destination visual containers a distinct clay-tablet appearance.
- **Pill Geometry:** Applied unconditionally to buttons, status indicators, travel tags, and price badges (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Image Masking:** Photography is enveloped in continuous squircle or pill-arched top borders (`border-radius: 32px 32px 18px 18px`) to maintain editorial elegance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), background in deep navy (`#153650`) with warm ivory typography (`#F8F6F0`). Dual ambient top sheen (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`) and tinted drop shadow (`0 8px 18px rgba(21,54,80,0.22)`). Hover smoothly lifts by 1px with increased shadow spread.
- **Secondary CTA:** Cream-clay pill button (`#F1EEE5`), text in `#153650`, with subtle pillowed inner highlight and perimeter ring (`1px solid rgba(21, 54, 80, 0.08)`).
- **Tertiary / Accent CTA:** Golden accent fill (`#E9B95E`) with `#153650` typography, reserved for instant quote requests and VIP booking actions.

### Destination & Itinerary Cards
- Rendered on a soft clay container (`#F8F6F0`) with a 2rem (`32px`) outer radius and dual light-source shadow.
- Photography occupies the top frame with an inner ambient glow to smooth the transition into the cream card body.
- Bottom card area displays editorial destination title in Playfair Display, alongside a floating pill badge showing duration and price in Warm Gold.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed neumorphic styling: background `#F1EEE5` with soft inner shadow (`inset 2px 2px 5px rgba(21,54,80,0.06)`).
- Text styled in Plus Jakarta Sans (`#153650`). Placeholder copy styled in muted navy (`rgba(21, 54, 80, 0.5)`).
- Focused state applies an outer glow in Ocean Blue (`#246B9B`, `0 0 0 3px rgba(36, 107, 155, 0.18)`).

### Chips & Filter Badges
- Continuous pill shape with subtle raised extrusion.
- Inactive state: `#F8F6F0` surface with faint border (`1px solid rgba(21,54,80,0.06)`).
- Active state: fills with Ocean Blue (`#246B9B`) and pure white text, accompanied by a soft blue ambient drop (`0 4px 12px rgba(36,107,155,0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular organic toggles with soft clay indents.
- Checked state reveals a smooth convex golden-yellow dot (`#E9B95E`) nestled within a navy ring (`#153650`).

### Itinerary Timeline Stepper
- Vertical connecting line drawn in soft cream sand (`#E8E3D5`, 2px thickness).
- Day indicator nodes styled as pillowed cream pebbles with Playfair Display numerals, glowing gold when marked as current or active day.
