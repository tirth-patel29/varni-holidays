## Product Purpose

Varni Holidays is an end-to-end luxury travel management platform and operations CRM headquartered in Anand, Gujarat. It bridges authentic regional hospitality with modern operational tooling—enabling customers to discover and request bespoke holidays, while empowering internal staff with a full-stack CRM, editable quotation engine, multi-page PDF generation, and automated consular milestone communications.

## Positioning

"Your Gateway to Joyful Holidays" — Authentic Gujarat hospitality rooted in Anand, paired with institutional-grade booking precision, transparent pricing without hidden markups, and end-to-end counselor guidance from initial inquiry to return flight.

## Operating Context

- **Public Client Portal:** Curated holiday packages (e.g. Scenic Kerala & Backwaters), hotel reservations, flight inquiries, travel insurance, and visa application assistance.
- **Admin & Employee CRM Desk:** Lead pipeline, customer dossiers, follow-up calendar, rate governance, quotation snapshots, and communication dispatch via Resend.
- **Consular Desk:** 9-stage visa lifecycle tracking, document vault (passports, financial records, approved visas), and automated client status updates.
- **Executive Synchronization:** Real-time transactional outbox mirroring into Google Sheets for executive overview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL database, Resend email API.
- **Design Language:** Sharp rectangular geometry, light editorial layout, luxury typography.
- **Key Constraints:**
  - Original supplier costs, passports, and margins are strictly permission-guarded and never exposed to unauthorized viewers or plain emails.
  - Consular decisions are never automatically inferred—they require verified staff updates.
  - Quotation PDF generation is executed server-side with immutable revision snapshots.

## Product Principles

1. **Flawless Information Density:** Essential travel data, pricing breakdowns, and status milestones must be immediately legible without frivolous decoration.
2. **Respect for Privacy & Sovereign Protocol:** Sensitive client credentials and embassy correspondence are shielded with strict encryption and physical collection workflows.
3. **Radical Reliability:** Every financial quotation, booking update, and visa communication is immutably snapshotted and audited.
4. **Authentic Hospitality:** Every customer communication carries warm, personal Gujarati hospitality signed by a real travel counselor.
